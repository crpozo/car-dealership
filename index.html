    How the colours work

    Green at or above the goal · Yellow still at or above the yellow band (default 85% of the goal) · Red below that. A metric with no goal set stays uncoloured — nothing is judged against an invented number.

    Pace uses Excel NETWORKDAYS across the selected month: workdays elapsed ÷ workdays total. There is no holiday calendar, so a dealership holiday reads as a worked day.

    Monthly sales goals

    Goals are not present in the CRM exports, so they start empty. A store with no goal shows “no goal” and is never coloured red.
